FUNCTION
POST /.netlify/functions/personalized-voice
Body: userId, fresh Meta nonce, name, language (fr/en), kind (win/lose/training),
revision (ps-voice-20261010-v2-mp3). No free text, model, voice or callback URL.
Response: bounded mono MP3, 44100 Hz / 96 kbps, <=20.1 seconds including encoder padding; private/no-store.
400 invalid input; 401 proof invalid; 409 reserved/failed generation;
429 quota; 503 disabled/unavailable. Unity always has an immediate generic fallback.
There is no GET endpoint exposing personalized files.

STORAGE / COST LIMITS
Postgres: short serialized transaction reserves job and budget before a paid call.
Cache bytes: separate private Netlify Blobs store project-s-personalized-voices-v1.
Limits: 6 generations/user/day, 12/user/month, 5000 prompt characters globally/day,
20000/month, 10000 total jobs. Both languages fit in the per-user daily allowance.
Function declares an edge rate limit of 30 requests/IP/domain/minute. Its deployed
enforcement must be checked in a preview; it is not a substitute for SQL quotas.
Failed/uncertain paid calls retain reservations and are not automatically retried.
A timeout/crash between saving a blob and marking it ready needs operator review;
it returns generic audio rather than risk another charge.
Ledger conservatively counts prompt characters, not a promise of exact provider
billing. The dedicated ElevenLabs hard cap is additional protection.
Successful cache entries are reused; new name/language/profile revision can require
new audio. No cache expiration triggers generation on every launch.
