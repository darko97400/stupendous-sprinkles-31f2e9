2. Data Collected and Processed

DEDALE VR is a free VR puzzle game with optional purchases of additional puzzle boxes and consumable hint credits. The application uses the Meta Horizon Platform features User ID and In-App Purchases and/or Downloadable Content (IAP).

Platform featureDataHow it is used
User ID The unique Meta Horizon User ID associated with the authenticated Meta Horizon account. Used only as necessary to support Meta Horizon in-app purchase functionality, including associating purchase and entitlement information with the correct authenticated account and supporting verification and restoration of eligible durable purchases.
In-App Purchases / Downloadable Content Purchase and entitlement information supplied through Meta Horizon, including information indicating whether the authenticated account owns or is entitled to specific DEDALE VR content. Used to process and verify optional purchases, unlock purchased puzzle boxes or hint credits, and restore access to eligible durable puzzle-box purchases.

3. User Profile Information

DEDALE VR does not use the Meta Horizon User Profile platform feature for its current functionality. DEDALE VR does not read, display, or store the user's Meta Horizon profile name or profile picture as part of its purchase system.

4. In-App Purchases

DEDALE VR can be played for free and offers optional purchases. Players may purchase additional puzzle boxes and consumable hint credits through Meta Horizon.

Meta Horizon processes the transaction. DEDALE VR uses purchase and entitlement information to determine whether purchased content should be unlocked and, for eligible durable puzzle-box purchases, restored.

DEDALE VR does not receive or store the user's full credit-card, debit-card, or other complete payment information.

5. Consumable Hint Credits

Hint credits are consumable purchases. The current in-game balance of hint credits is stored locally on the user's device. DEDALE VR does not currently guarantee transfer of unused hint-credit balances between devices or restoration of the local balance after the application's local data is completely deleted or after a complete reinstallation.

6. Purpose of Data Processing

The User ID and IAP information described above are processed only as necessary to provide purchase-related functionality, including:

  • processing and verifying purchases;
  • checking purchase or entitlement status;
  • unlocking purchased puzzle boxes;
  • providing purchased hint credits;
  • restoring eligible durable puzzle-box purchases; and
  • helping prevent unauthorized access to paid content.

DEDALE VR does not sell Meta Horizon user data and does not use this data for targeted advertising.

7. Data Storage and Retention

DEDALE VR retains personal data under its control only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy or when retention is required by applicable law.

Some Meta Horizon data may be processed temporarily and may not be independently stored by DEDALE VR. Purchase records and other information stored and controlled by Meta Horizon remain subject to Meta's own privacy, account, and retention practices.

8. Data Sharing

DEDALE VR does not sell personal data. Data may be processed through Meta Horizon services as necessary to provide the purchase functionality requested by the user. Data may also be disclosed when required by applicable law or when reasonably necessary to protect the security of the application or its users.

9. Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards are used to protect personal data under DEDALE VR's control against unauthorized access, alteration, misuse, loss, or disclosure.

10. User Data Deletion

All DEDALE VR users may request deletion of personal data associated with DEDALE VR, regardless of their country, region, nationality, or place of residence. There is no fee to submit a deletion request.

To request deletion, email:

xperiencefx@gmail.com
Subject: DEDALE VR – Data Deletion Request

Please provide sufficient information to identify the data associated with the request, such as the Meta Horizon User ID where available. Do not provide a Meta password or full payment-card information.

After receiving a valid request, DEDALE VR will delete personal data under its control associated with the requesting user, or confirm when DEDALE VR does not independently retain personal data associated with that user.

If requested information is stored and controlled solely by Meta Horizon and is not accessible to or controlled by DEDALE VR, the user may also need to use Meta's own account or privacy tools to request deletion of that Meta-controlled information. This does not restrict the user's ability to request deletion of data controlled by DEDALE VR.

Deletion may be limited only where retention is required by applicable law or reasonably necessary to prevent fraud or abuse, resolve disputes, or establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

11. Children's Privacy

DEDALE VR does not intentionally collect personal data from children independently of the Meta Horizon Platform features described in this policy. Use of Meta Horizon accounts and devices is also subject to Meta's applicable age requirements and parental supervision policies.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if DEDALE VR's functionality, data practices, legal requirements, or Meta Horizon integrations change. Updates will be published at the same public Privacy Policy URL with a revised “Last updated” date.
