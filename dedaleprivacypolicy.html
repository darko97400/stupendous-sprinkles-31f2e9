2. Data Processed by Dedale VR

Meta Horizon User ID

Dedale VR may process the unique Meta Horizon User ID associated with the current user.

The User ID is used only to identify the Meta Horizon account when required for Meta platform functionality, including in-app purchases, purchase entitlement verification and restoration of purchased content.

Dedale VR does not use the Meta Horizon User ID for advertising, marketing, behavioral profiling or selling user data.

Meta Horizon User Profile

When Meta Horizon User Profile access is enabled for the application, Meta Platform services may make limited profile information available to the application, such as the user's Meta Horizon display name, username or profile image URL.

Dedale VR does not use profile information for advertising, marketing, social profiling or analytics.

Dedale VR does not maintain an external developer-operated database containing Meta Horizon usernames, display names or profile photos.

Profile information is processed only when made available by the Meta Horizon Platform SDK as part of the platform account context used by the application.

In-App Purchase Information

Dedale VR uses Meta Horizon In-App Purchases to allow users to purchase additional puzzle boxes, downloadable content and hint credits.

The application may receive or process information related to these purchases, including:

  • product or SKU identifier;
  • purchase status;
  • entitlement or ownership status;
  • information necessary to restore purchased content.

This information is used only to:

  • complete in-app purchases;
  • unlock purchased content;
  • verify ownership of purchased content;
  • restore eligible purchases;
  • prevent unauthorized access to paid content.

Payments are processed by Meta Horizon or its authorized payment providers.

Dedale VR does not receive or store users' payment card numbers, bank account information or other full payment credentials.

3. How User Data Is Used

Meta Horizon user data processed by Dedale VR is used exclusively to provide functionality requested by the user, including purchasing, unlocking and restoring in-app content.

Dedale VR does not use Meta Horizon user data for:

  • targeted advertising;
  • marketing;
  • behavioral profiling;
  • selling personal information;
  • cross-app tracking.

4. Data Storage

Dedale VR does not maintain a developer-operated external user database containing Meta Horizon User IDs, Meta Horizon profiles or purchase histories.

Purchase and entitlement information may be requested from Meta Horizon services when necessary to determine whether content has been purchased by the current Meta Horizon account.

Certain game information may also be stored locally on the user's device.

Meta Horizon maintains its own records relating to Meta accounts and transactions according to Meta's own privacy policies and legal obligations.

5. Data Sharing

Dedale VR does not sell user data.

User information is not shared with advertisers or data brokers.

Limited information may be processed by Meta Horizon as necessary to provide account, platform, commerce and in-app purchase functionality.

6. Data Retention

Dedale VR retains user data under its direct control only for as long as necessary to provide the requested functionality or comply with applicable legal obligations.

Dedale VR does not maintain a permanent external database of Meta Horizon User IDs, user profiles or purchase histories.

Transaction and entitlement records maintained directly by Meta Horizon are subject to Meta's own retention and privacy policies.

7. Data Deletion Requests

Users may request deletion of personal data controlled by Dedale VR at any time.

To request deletion, send an email to: xperiencefx@gmail.com

Subject: Dedale VR Data Deletion Request

The request should identify Dedale VR and provide enough information for us to identify any data associated with the request.

We will review and process valid deletion requests within 30 days, unless a longer retention period is required by law.

If Dedale VR holds user data under its direct control, that data will be deleted or anonymized where legally permitted.

If no user data is stored on Dedale VR-operated systems, we will confirm this to the requester.

Information controlled exclusively by Meta Horizon, including Meta account information and Meta transaction records, cannot be deleted directly by Dedale VR. Users wishing to delete information controlled by Meta must use Meta's account and privacy tools or contact Meta directly.

Users can also remove locally stored Dedale VR application data by uninstalling the application or clearing the application's stored data from their device where supported.

8. Children's Privacy

Use of Meta Horizon accounts and devices remains subject to Meta's age requirements, parental controls and applicable laws.

10. International Processing
