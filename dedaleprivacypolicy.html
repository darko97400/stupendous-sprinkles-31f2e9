Privacy Policy – DEDALE VR

Effective date: August 26, 2026

Last updated: August 26, 2026

DEDALE VR uses Meta Horizon Platform services, including Meta Horizon Add-ons and In-App Purchases. To provide these features, DEDALE VR collects and processes limited Meta Horizon user data: Meta Horizon User ID, Meta Horizon User Profile information, and In-App Purchase information.

This Privacy Policy explains exactly what data is processed, why it is processed, how it is used, how it is retained, and how users can request deletion of data controlled by DEDALE VR.

2. User Data Collected and Processed

2.1 Meta Horizon User ID

DEDALE VR collects and processes the unique Meta Horizon User ID made available by Meta Horizon Platform services for the current user.

The User ID is used only to:

  • identify the Meta Horizon account across sessions where required for platform functionality;
  • associate the current Meta Horizon account with purchased content;
  • verify purchase entitlements and ownership;
  • unlock purchased puzzle boxes or other purchased content; and
  • restore eligible purchases.

DEDALE VR does not use the Meta Horizon User ID for advertising, marketing, behavioral profiling, or selling personal data.

2.2 Meta Horizon User Profile

DEDALE VR collects and processes limited Meta Horizon User Profile information made available through Meta Horizon Platform services. This information can include the user's Meta Horizon username or display name and profile image URL.

This profile information is processed only as part of the Meta Horizon account context used by DEDALE VR for Meta Horizon platform functionality associated with Add-ons and In-App Purchases and to identify the current user across sessions where required by those platform services.

DEDALE VR does not use Meta Horizon User Profile information for advertising, marketing, behavioral profiling, or selling personal data. DEDALE VR does not maintain a developer-operated external database containing users' Meta Horizon usernames, display names, or profile images.

2.3 In-App Purchase Information

DEDALE VR collects and processes In-App Purchase information provided through Meta Horizon when a user purchases, verifies, unlocks, or restores paid content.

This information can include:

  • the product or SKU identifier;
  • purchase status;
  • entitlement or ownership status; and
  • information required to verify or restore eligible purchased content.

This information is used only to:

  • complete and support in-app purchases;
  • provide purchased puzzle boxes and hint credits;
  • verify ownership of purchased content;
  • restore eligible purchases; and
  • prevent unauthorized access to paid content.

Payments are handled by Meta Horizon and its payment providers. DEDALE VR does not receive or store payment card numbers, bank account numbers, or full payment credentials.

3. When This Data Is Processed

Meta Horizon User ID and User Profile information are processed when DEDALE VR initializes or uses Meta Horizon Platform services that require the current Meta Horizon account context. In-App Purchase information is processed when a user purchases content or when DEDALE VR verifies, unlocks, or restores purchased content.

4. How the Data Is Used

The Meta Horizon data described above is used only to operate and support the Meta Horizon platform and commerce features requested by the user, including account identification for those features, purchases, entitlement verification, unlocking content, and restoring eligible purchases.

DEDALE VR does not use this Meta Horizon user data for:

  • targeted advertising;
  • marketing;
  • behavioral profiling;
  • cross-app tracking; or
  • selling personal information.

5. Storage and Retention

DEDALE VR does not maintain a developer-operated external database containing Meta Horizon User IDs, Meta Horizon User Profile information, or Meta Horizon purchase histories.

Data received from Meta Horizon Platform services is processed only as necessary to provide the functionality described in this policy. Purchase and entitlement information may be requested again from Meta Horizon when needed to verify or restore purchased content.

Certain game information, such as locally saved game progress or locally saved consumable-game state, may be stored on the user's device. This local information is not stored in a DEDALE VR-operated external user database.

Meta may independently retain account, transaction, entitlement, and other platform records in accordance with Meta's own policies and legal obligations.

6. Sharing and Third-Party Processing

DEDALE VR does not sell Meta Horizon user data and does not share it with advertisers or data brokers.

Meta Platforms Technologies and its affiliates or service providers may collect, receive, or process information as necessary to provide Meta Horizon account, platform, commerce, entitlement, and In-App Purchase services.

7. Data Deletion Requests

All users, regardless of country or region, may request deletion of personal data collected or stored under DEDALE VR's control.

To submit a deletion request, email: xperiencefx@gmail.com

Use the subject line: DEDALE VR Data Deletion Request.

Please state that your request concerns DEDALE VR and provide only the information reasonably necessary for us to identify any data associated with your request.

DEDALE VR will review valid deletion requests and delete or anonymize personal data under its control within 30 days, unless retention is required by applicable law. If DEDALE VR does not hold any server-side personal data associated with the request, we will confirm that to the requester.

Users can delete locally stored DEDALE VR application data by uninstalling the application or clearing the application's stored data where supported by the device.

Meta controls its own Meta Horizon account, transaction, purchase, entitlement, and platform records. Requests concerning data controlled by Meta must be submitted through Meta's own privacy and account-management mechanisms.

8. Security

DEDALE VR uses reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect data processed under its control. No electronic system can guarantee absolute security.
