DedaleVR does not independently collect names, postal addresses, passwords, payment card information, precise location data, microphone recordings, camera recordings, or advertising profiles.

3. How Information Is Used

Limited platform information is used only to:

  • Verify purchases and ownership of downloadable content.
  • Unlock purchased puzzle packs or additional game content.
  • Restore purchases on supported devices.
  • Provide and maintain the core functionality of DedaleVR.
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, or unauthorized access to paid content.

DedaleVR does not use Meta Horizon User Data or Device User Data for targeted advertising.

4. Purchases and Payments

Purchases are processed by Meta Horizon or its authorized payment providers. DedaleVR does not directly receive or store your full payment card details. Meta's own privacy terms apply to information processed by Meta during a transaction.

5. Data Sharing

DedaleVR does not sell personal information.

  • To service providers acting on behalf of the data controller, if such providers are introduced in the future.
