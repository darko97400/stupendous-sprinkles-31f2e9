2. Information DEDALE VR May Process

DEDALE VR may process limited information supplied through Meta Horizon Platform features that are enabled for the application. Depending on the feature being used, this may include:

  • Meta Horizon User ID / platform identifier – used to associate the current Meta Horizon user with platform functionality.
  • User profile information – such as the user's Meta Horizon display name and profile picture, when provided through the Meta Horizon User Profile feature.
  • Purchase, entitlement, downloadable-content, or add-on information – used to verify whether a user owns content and to unlock or restore that content.
  • Limited technical information required to operate the application and communicate with supported Meta Horizon platform services.

DEDALE VR does not independently request or collect postal addresses, Meta account passwords, full payment-card information, precise location data, camera recordings, microphone recordings, or advertising profiles unless this Privacy Policy is updated before such collection begins.

3. How the Information Is Used

The information described above is processed only as needed to provide DEDALE VR and its Meta Horizon platform functionality, including to:

  • identify the current Meta Horizon user for supported platform functions;
  • display supported Meta Horizon profile information inside the application where applicable;
  • verify ownership of in-app purchases, downloadable content, puzzle packs, or add-ons;
  • unlock and restore purchased content;
  • provide, maintain, secure, and troubleshoot the application's core functionality; and
  • prevent fraud, abuse, or unauthorized access to paid content.

DEDALE VR does not use Meta Horizon user data for targeted advertising and does not sell personal information.

4. Purchases and Payments

Payments are processed by Meta Horizon or its authorized payment providers. DEDALE VR does not receive or store full payment-card details. Meta's own privacy terms apply to information processed directly by Meta as part of a transaction.

5. Data Storage and Retention

DEDALE VR only retains user data under its control for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, or when retention is required by applicable law.

Some Meta Horizon platform information may be processed temporarily and may not be stored independently by DEDALE VR. Information controlled solely by Meta Horizon remains subject to Meta's own systems, retention practices, and privacy policies.

6. Data Sharing

DEDALE VR does not sell personal data. User data may be shared only when necessary to operate the application, comply with a valid legal obligation, protect the security of the application or its users, or interact with Meta Horizon platform services requested by the application.

If DEDALE VR begins using an additional third-party service that receives personal data, this Privacy Policy will be updated to describe that service and the relevant data use.

7. Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards are used to protect information under DEDALE VR's control against unauthorized access, misuse, alteration, loss, or disclosure. No electronic system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Children's Privacy

DEDALE VR does not intentionally collect personal information from children independently of the Meta Horizon platform features described in this policy. Use of Meta Horizon devices and accounts is also subject to Meta's applicable age requirements, parental supervision features, and policies.

9. User Data Deletion – Available to All Users Worldwide

Every DEDALE VR user may request deletion of user data associated with DEDALE VR, regardless of country, region, nationality, or place of residence. There is no fee to make a deletion request.

To request deletion of user data associated with DEDALE VR, send an email to:

xperiencefx@gmail.com
Suggested subject: DEDALE VR – Data Deletion Request

Please provide enough information to identify the data associated with your request, such as your Meta Horizon User ID or Meta Horizon display name when applicable. Do not send your Meta password or payment-card information.

After receiving a valid deletion request, DEDALE VR will delete personal data under its control that is associated with the requester, or will confirm that DEDALE VR does not independently store personal data associated with that user. Requests will be handled within a reasonable period.

A deletion request may be limited only where specific data must be retained to comply with an applicable legal obligation, resolve a dispute, prevent fraud or abuse, or establish, exercise, or defend legal claims. Where applicable, only the minimum information necessary for that purpose will be retained for the required period.

If requested information is stored and controlled solely by Meta Horizon and is not accessible to or controlled by DEDALE VR, the user may also need to use Meta's own privacy or account tools to request deletion of that Meta-controlled information. This does not limit the user's right to request deletion of any data controlled by DEDALE VR.
