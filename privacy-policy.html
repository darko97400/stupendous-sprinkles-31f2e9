Privacy
Policy.

This policy explains how Project S handles limited player data used to provide online gameplay and optional personalized announcements on Meta Quest and PICO.

Publisher: XFX StudioUpdated: November 23, 2026

Information we may collect

Meta Quest. Project S may use your Meta Horizon display name, platform identifier, multiplayer session information and gameplay statistics such as scores, destroyed robots or match results to provide online game features.

PICO. If you choose personalized announcements and grant profile access, Project S uses your PICO display name and a platform token that verifies your account. The game can use a standard announcement if you decline profile access or personalization is unavailable.

How information is used

Project S uses gameplay information to display leaderboards and statistics, support matchmaking and online sessions, and operate game services. For personalized announcements, our server verifies the platform token and uses a pseudonymous account reference to limit repeat requests and manage the service. ElevenLabs receives the scripted text, which may contain your display name, and the selected language to generate the announcement. We do not send your platform token or microphone audio to ElevenLabs.

Service providers

Netlify hosts the website and supports online game features. The generated announcements are cached in Netlify Blobs in the United States (Ohio). The voice service stores pseudonymous usage records in its database. ElevenLabs generates announcements using its US service. Photon provides online multiplayer sessions, player connection and matchmaking. These services may process data outside your country.

Advertising and data sale

Project S does not sell user data, use it for third-party advertising, or use third-party advertising SDKs or external tracking tools.

Data retention and deletion

Gameplay information, usage records and generated announcements may be retained as needed to operate the associated features and reuse announcements. To request access to or deletion of associated data, email xperiencefx@gmail.com and include your platform and the display name you used in Project S.

Children’s privacy

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information through Project S, contact us so we can review the request and remove associated data as appropriate.

Policy changes

This policy may be updated when game features, service providers or data practices change.
