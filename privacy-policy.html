Privacy
Policy.

This policy explains how Project S handles limited player data used to provide online gameplay features.

Publisher: XFX StudioUpdated: May 28, 2026

Information we may collect

Project S may use limited Meta Horizon / Meta Quest data only when needed for game features. This may include your profile or display name, a platform identifier, multiplayer session information and gameplay statistics such as scores, destroyed robots or match results.

How information is used

This information is used to display leaderboards and statistics, connect players, support matchmaking and online sessions, and maintain player-facing game services.

Service providers

Netlify hosts the official website and may support leaderboard services. Photon provides multiplayer sessions, player connection and matchmaking. These providers process limited information only for the features described here.

Advertising and data sale

Project S does not sell user data, use it for third-party advertising, or use third-party advertising SDKs or external tracking tools.

Data retention and deletion

Gameplay-related information may remain stored for as long as needed to provide online features. To request deletion of associated data, email xperiencefx@gmail.com and include the Meta/Oculus display name used in Project S.

Children’s privacy

Project S does not knowingly collect children’s personal information beyond what is required by the Meta Quest platform and gameplay features. Contact us if you believe information should be removed.

Policy changes

This policy may be updated when game features, service providers or data practices change.
